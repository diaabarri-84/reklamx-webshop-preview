# Product art

These are interim illustrations, not photographs. They are drawn placeholders so that a
product without a picture still looks deliberate on the page, and none of them shows the
shop's own goods — they are generic vector drawings on the shop's neumorphic surface.

Four views ship per product (`tshirt-1.svg` … `tshirt-4.svg`, and so on). Reference them
as `/product-art/<name>.svg`. Replace them one product at a time from the dashboard's
image field as soon as real photography exists; nothing else needs changing.
